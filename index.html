AUTOLOOPS

It keeps working after the chat closes.

Most agents stop when you do. Spark's autoloops keep your workflows running in the background, on your machine, on your schedule.

  • 01

    Loops run between sessions

    Research briefs, drafts, monitoring, reporting. The work you repeat keeps moving while you do something else.

  • 02

    Every run leaves a trace

    What it read, what it decided, why. You audit the loop the way you'd audit a teammate. Not a black box.

  • 03

    You are the gate

    When a run finds an improvement, Spark proposes it. Nothing changes your workflow without your approval.

BENCHMARKS

Nothing is kept without proof.

Self-improvement is easy to claim and hard to govern. Spark's answer is benchmarks: every candidate change runs against your bar before it touches your workflow.

  • 01

    Your bar, not ours

    You define what a good result looks like for each workflow. Spark scores every run against it.

  • 02

    Tested before trusted

    Candidates run against held-out cases, trap cases, and a no-op guard, so a lucky run can't sneak in as progress.

  • 03

    No blind self-improvement

    No unreviewed memory either. Promotion happens with you in the loop, and everything is replayable.
