005

The brain is built to
think. Not to guess.

Spark doesn't just call an LLM and hope. The research engine works methodically: it gathers what it knows, reasons through it step by step, scores the result, and shows its work. Every loop also learns. The same mistake never costs you twice.

gather reason score show work learn
000loops
running
  1. 01

    Gather

    Pull only the context this task needs. Memory, chips, tools, docs. Nothing extra. Nothing that leaks into the wrong mission.

  2. 02

    Reason

    Break the problem into steps the board can show. Each step has an input, an output, and a reason it was picked.

  3. 03

    Score

    Every candidate answer is rated against the success criteria you set. Bad ones get sent back. Good ones get kept.

  4. 04

    Show work

    You see why Spark answered, not just what. Every edge of the graph has a rationale attached. Replayable forever.

  5. 05

    Learn

    What worked gets promoted. What didn't gets a watch-out. The next mission starts smarter than the last one, without you lifting a finger.

007the part that matters most

It doesn't take over
your computer.

Other agents work by asking for unlimited access. Your files, your terminal, your everything. Spark goes the other way. It works through a browser extension, your normal app logins, and the modules you chose to install. If you didn't give Spark a way to do something, it can't do it.

That's not a limitation. That's the point.

◇ Scoped Every chip declares its permissions in spark.toml. You approve them once. You revoke them anytime.
◈ Auditable Every action Spark takes is logged locally. You can replay any mission line by line.
◉ Revocable Uninstall a chip and its access vanishes with it. No orphan tokens. No background processes.
◐ Yours Your agent, your data, your rules. If you turn off the internet, Spark still works.
