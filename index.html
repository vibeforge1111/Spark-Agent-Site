Every other AI agent wants the keys to everything. Your files, your terminal, your whole machine. That's why half the people who install them never actually use them for anything important. Spark only sees what you already showed it, through the browser extension and the apps you already trust. Your work stays yours. Your terminal stays yours. Your sleep stays yours.

That's not a limitation. That's the whole reason it works.

◇ Scoped Every chip declares its permissions in spark.toml. You approve them once. You revoke them anytime.
◈ Auditable Every action Spark takes is logged locally. You can replay any mission line by line.
◉ Revocable Uninstall a chip and its access vanishes with it. No orphan tokens. No background processes.
◐ Yours Your agent, your data, your rules. If you turn off the internet, Spark still works.
