Agent-readable

Turn observations into a useful report.

Do not just say "this is broken." Include the repo, page or command, expected behavior, actual behavior, reproduction steps, safe logs, and the smallest suggested improvement.

Opening a repo link sends you to GitHub. Sign in there, then create the issue or PR with secrets removed.

What high-signal feedback looks like.

Good feedback gives a maintainer or agent enough signal to act without guessing. It is specific, private by default, and small enough to turn into a check or PR.

01

Clear

Point at the exact page, command, module, or repo.

02

Safe

Remove keys, tokens, private memory, names, and raw logs.

03

Useful

Name the smallest change, screenshot, command, or test.
